Hermes

A personal, single-user automation client.

Hermes is a private desktop assistant that runs on the account owner's own computer. It is not a public service, has no sign-up, and serves exactly one person: its owner.

What it does

  • Reads and drafts email, and manages calendar events, for its owner only
  • Reads and edits the owner's own Google Docs, Sheets and Drive files
  • Organises notes, tasks and project information stored locally on the owner's machine

Google user data

When the owner authorises the app, it requests Google Workspace scopes so it can act on the owner's behalf. Data is processed locally on the owner's device and is never sold, shared, or transferred to third parties. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.
